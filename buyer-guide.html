What to include in an agricultural sourcing enquiry.

A practical checklist for businesses approaching a farm or agricultural supplier.

Buyer Resources · Buyer Guide

A clear first enquiry can save time on both sides. You do not need a long specification document — just enough context to understand the requirement.

1. Start with the product

State the agricultural product you want and, where relevant, the form you need. GUDDA FARM’s current website range includes Fresh Turmeric and Raw Turmeric.

2. Add quantity

Give a target or approximate quantity. If the requirement is recurring, you can mention that in the message as well.

3. Give the destination

City, state or country is useful context for the supply conversation.

4. Explain your business use

Tell the supplier whether you are a trader, processor, distributor, brand, retailer or another type of buyer.

5. Add requirements you already know

If you have quality, packing, timing or other requirements, include them. If you are unsure, simply say so and ask what information is needed.
