Buyer questions

Before you enquire.

Can I enquire for bulk quantities?

Yes. The buyer enquiry form is designed for bulk requirements. Share the quantity you are considering and we can discuss the requirement.

Can I ask about a product not shown on the site?

Yes. Tell us what agricultural produce you are looking for and we can discuss whether it fits our sourcing range.

How do I start?

Use the Bulk Buyer page or the WhatsApp button and send your product, quantity, destination and requirements.

Do you publish fixed prices or minimum quantities?

We do not publish assumptions on this site. Requirements can vary, so we prefer to discuss the specific enquiry directly.
